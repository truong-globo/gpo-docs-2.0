## The words we use

These terms appear on nearly every page. This table is the short version — each has a page of its own if you want more.

<table><thead><tr><th width="210">Term</th><th>Means</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td><strong>Option set</strong></td><td>A group of options, plus the rules for which products, customers, and countries it applies to. This is the thing you create, save, and activate.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Option</strong></td><td>One field inside an option set — a text box, a dropdown, a swatch row, a divider. There are 32 kinds.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Option value</strong></td><td>One of the choices inside a selection-style option. A dropdown with four entries has four option values.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Section</strong></td><td>A container that groups options together under a heading, optionally collapsible.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Label</strong></td><td>The text a shopper reads above an option on the product page.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Name</strong></td><td>The internal name of an option. It is what appears on the cart, at checkout, and on the order.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Add-on</strong></td><td>An extra charge attached to an option or to one of its values.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Add-on product</strong></td><td>A real Shopify product used to carry an add-on's price and stock.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Product rule</strong></td><td>How an option set decides which products it applies to: by hand, by condition, or all of them.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>App embed</strong></td><td>The switch in your theme that lets the app run on your storefront.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>App block</strong></td><td>An optional theme block that pins the widget to an exact position.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Widget</strong></td><td>The rendered option form as shoppers see it on your storefront.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Personalizer</strong></td><td>The live preview that draws the customer's text or image onto the product photo.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Template</strong></td><td>A saved option set you can copy, either one of ours or one of yours.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Workflow</strong></td><td>An automation that runs after an order is placed.</td></tr></tbody></table>

A longer alphabetical list lives in the [Glossary](../help/glossary.md).
