# Monster Cart

[Monster Cart Upsell+ Free Gift](https://apps.shopify.com/monster-upsells) is recognized automatically. There is nothing to connect and no selector to enter.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Set it up first.** The two settings to change are the same for every cart drawer app, and they are on [Cart drawer apps](./#set-up). This page covers what is specific to Monster Cart.
{% endhint %}

## Merging add-ons is no longer required

{% hint style="success" %}
**If support previously told you to turn on Merge Main product & Add-on products for this app, you can turn it back off.**
{% endhint %}

The Monster Cart drawer used to open only when **Merge Main product & Add-on products** was on, because it could not handle several linked lines being added at once. That restriction is gone.

Adding to cart now adds every add-on product and opens the drawer, with the add-on lines displayed separately — each priced on its own, and each without a quantity box or a Remove button.

Merging is now a presentation choice like any other:

<table><thead><tr><th width="230">Merge setting</th><th>In the Monster Cart drawer</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td><strong>Off</strong> (default on new stores)</td><td>The main item and one line per add-on product, each with its own price</td></tr><tr><td><strong>On</strong></td><td>One combined item, with the add-ons presented as part of it</td></tr></tbody></table>

Both work. The setting is in **Settings** > **Settings** > **Add-on price**. See [Merge main product and add-ons](../../add-on-pricing/merge-as-bundle.md).

## Frequently Bought Together

Monster Cart's upsells are shown as **Frequently Bought Together** in the drawer. They behave like any drawer upsell: one click adds the product to the cart, which skips your option form.

The app leaves your generated add-on products out of that list on its own. For products that do have options, turn on **Send cart drawer app upsells to the product page**. See [Upsells in the drawer skip your option form](./#upsells-in-the-drawer-skip-your-option-form).
