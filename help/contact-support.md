# Contact support

## How to reach us

<table><thead><tr><th width="200">Channel</th><th width="250">Where</th><th>Best for</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td><strong>Live chat</strong></td><td>The chat bubble inside the app</td><td>Quick questions, and anything where a screenshot helps</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Email</strong></td><td><a href="mailto:contact@globo.io">contact@globo.io</a></td><td>Anything needing detail, files, or a longer explanation</td></tr></tbody></table>

Live chat is quickest for a straight question. Email is better when the answer depends on your setup, because you can attach screenshots and we can reply with a proper walkthrough.

## What to include

The difference between a one-reply answer and four days of back and forth is almost always the first message. Include as much of this as applies:

<table><thead><tr><th width="290">Always</th><th>Why</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Your store's <code>.myshopify.com</code> address</td><td>So we can look at your actual configuration</td></tr><tr><td>Your theme's name</td><td>Most storefront problems are theme-specific</td></tr><tr><td>The name of the option set involved</td><td>So we look at the right one</td></tr><tr><td>A link to the product page where it happens</td><td>So we see what you see</td></tr><tr><td>What you expected, and what happened instead</td><td>Two sentences saves a round trip</td></tr></tbody></table>

<table><thead><tr><th width="290">When it applies</th><th>Why</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Your storefront password</td><td>A password-protected store cannot be opened without it</td></tr><tr><td>A screenshot or short screen recording</td><td>Especially for anything visual or intermittent</td></tr><tr><td>An order number</td><td>For anything about orders, charges, or fulfilment</td></tr><tr><td>Which browser and device</td><td>For anything that happens on one and not another</td></tr><tr><td>Roughly when it started</td><td>Often lines up with a theme publish or a plan change</td></tr><tr><td>Which troubleshooting steps you have already tried</td><td>So we do not send you back over ground you have covered</td></tr></tbody></table>

{% hint style="info" %}
**Permission to look.** For anything on your storefront, saying we may open your store and view the product page in question gets us moving without an extra exchange. We will always ask before changing anything.
{% endhint %}

## Check these first

Most reports resolve to one of six things. Two minutes here often saves a wait:

<table><thead><tr><th width="330">If</th><th>Read</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Options are not appearing at all</td><td><a href="troubleshooting-not-showing.md">Options are not showing up</a></td></tr><tr><td>A charge is wrong, missing, or doubled</td><td><a href="troubleshooting-pricing.md">Pricing and add-on problems</a></td></tr><tr><td>Add to cart does nothing</td><td><a href="troubleshooting-cart-checkout.md">Cart and checkout problems</a></td></tr><tr><td>The live preview is not behaving</td><td><a href="troubleshooting-personalizer.md">Personalizer problems</a></td></tr><tr><td>Options show or hide at the wrong moment</td><td><a href="../conditional-logic/troubleshooting.md">Troubleshooting conditional logic</a></td></tr><tr><td>You want to know whether something is possible</td><td><a href="faq.md">FAQ</a></td></tr></tbody></table>

And the single most common cause of all: **the app embed is not enabled on the theme you just published.** It is per theme, so publishing a theme turns it off again. See [Enable the app embed](../getting-started/enable-the-app-embed.md).

## Custom work

If you need something the app does not do — a bespoke layout, an unusual pricing rule, an integration with a system of yours — say so and describe what you are trying to achieve, including the commercial reason. Some requests are already possible in a way that is not obvious; others are worth adding.

## Before you delete anything

Deleting an option set is permanent, and support cannot restore one.

Export it to CSV first. It takes seconds and gives you a file you can import back. See [Import and export](../option-sets/import-and-export.md) and [Duplicate and delete](../option-sets/duplicate-and-delete.md).
