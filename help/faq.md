# FAQ

<details>
<summary>Do I need to edit my theme's code?</summary>

No. Options render through a Shopify theme app embed, which you switch on from the theme editor — no code editing, and nothing left behind if you uninstall. See [Enable the app embed](../getting-started/enable-the-app-embed.md).

The only exception is printing option details on a custom packing slip, which needs a small snippet in that template. See [Show options on orders](../storefront/show-options-on-orders.md).
</details>

<details>
<summary>Will it work with my theme?</summary>

The app is built for Online Store 2.0 themes, which covers everything in Shopify's current theme store and most themes sold elsewhere. **Match theme style** carries ready-made styling for a long list of popular themes and falls back to your own colour and font settings for the rest. See [Match your theme style](../storefront/match-your-theme-style.md).
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can one option set apply to more than one product?</summary>

Yes. Use **Automatic Rules** to match products by tag, type, vendor, price, or collection, or **Apply to All Products** for your whole catalogue. Several option sets can also apply to the same product — they all render. See [Assign to products](../option-sets/assign-to-products.md).
</details>

<details>
<summary>Does add-on pricing really change what the customer pays?</summary>

Yes. The product page shows a preview, and the real amount is applied at checkout by Shopify itself, so it cannot be tampered with in the browser. See [How pricing is applied](../add-on-pricing/how-pricing-is-applied.md).
</details>

<details>
<summary>What is the difference between the three add-on modes?</summary>

**Add price** adds money with no product behind it — simplest, but no stock and no POS support. **Use existing product** links to something you already sell, so stock and SKU are real. **Automatically generate a product** has the app create the product for you. Services want the first; anything physical wants one of the other two. See [Add-on pricing](../add-on-pricing/README.md).
</details>

<details>
<summary>Why is gift wrap being charged five times?</summary>

The add-on is on **Default** mode, which follows the product quantity. Anything charged once per order belongs on **One time charge**. See [Advanced add-on modes](../add-on-pricing/advanced-add-on-modes.md).
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can options react to the Shopify variant the customer picked?</summary>

Yes. Alongside conditions on your own options, you can build conditions on the product's Shopify variants — so choosing `Large` in your theme's variant picker can reveal an option in the app's widget. See [Conditions on Shopify variants](../conditional-logic/conditions-on-shopify-variants.md).
</details>

<details>
<summary>Is a hidden required option still enforced?</summary>

No, and neither is it charged. Hidden options are skipped deliberately — otherwise a hidden required field would make the product unbuyable.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can customers see their personalisation before they buy?</summary>

Yes. The Personalizer draws their text and images onto your product photo live, as they type, and the finished design reaches the order so you can produce it. See [Product Personalizer](../personalizer/README.md).
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can customers fix a typo after adding to cart?</summary>

Yes, if you turn on **Show "Edit Options" button in cart**. For personalised products this noticeably reduces abandoned carts. See [Cart page](../storefront/cart-page.md).
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I move option sets from another product options app?</summary>

Yes. The importer reads CSV files exported from several other option apps as well as this app's own format. It is also how you copy option sets between your own stores. See [Import and export](../option-sets/import-and-export.md).
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I translate my options?</summary>

Yes — labels, values, help text, placeholders, and the widget's own wording, per storefront language, including right-to-left languages. See [Translations](../translations/README.md).
</details>

<details>
<summary>What happens to my work if I downgrade?</summary>

Nothing is deleted, but features the lower plan does not include stop working on your storefront straight away — including add-on charges. Read [Change your plan](../plans/change-your-plan.md) before you do it.
</details>
