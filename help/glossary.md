# Glossary

Terms from the app and from Shopify, in one alphabetical list. Where a term has a page of its own, it is linked.

## A

**Add-on**\
Any extra charge attached to an option or an option value. Set up in one of three modes — see [Add-on pricing](../add-on-pricing/README.md).

**Add-on product**\
A real Shopify product that carries an add-on charge, either one you already sell or one the app generated. It gives the add-on stock, a SKU, and a weight.

**Advanced settings** (add-on)\
The dropdown deciding how an add-on charge scales with quantity — per item, once per order, per character, and five more. See [Advanced add-on modes](../add-on-pricing/advanced-add-on-modes.md).

**App block**\
An optional block you place in your product template to pin the widget to an exact position. See [Add the app block](../getting-started/add-the-app-block.md).

**App embed**\
The Shopify mechanism that switches the app on for a theme. Nothing renders without it, and it is per theme. See [Enable the app embed](../getting-started/enable-the-app-embed.md).

**Automatic placement**\
The app choosing where to put the widget on your product page, used when you have not placed an app block. See [Widget placement](../storefront/widget-placement.md).

**Auto-fit**\
A Personalizer setting that shrinks text so it stays inside a set width instead of running off the product. See [Curve and auto-fit](../personalizer/curve-and-auto-fit.md).

## C

**Character counter**\
The `12 / 30` indicator under a text field. See [Limits](../option-types/shared-settings/limits.md#character-counter).

**Clip area**\
A boundary on the Personalizer canvas that a layer cannot be dragged outside. Essential whenever customers can move layers. See [Clip area](../personalizer/clip-area.md).

**Collapsible**\
A section or option that starts folded and opens when selected. See [Collapsible layouts and sliders](../option-types/shared-settings/collapsible-layouts-and-sliders.md).

**Conditional logic**\
Rules that show or hide an option based on earlier answers, or on the product's Shopify variant. See [Conditional logic](../conditional-logic/README.md).

**Country rule**\
A rule limiting an option set to visitors in particular countries. See [Assign to countries](../option-sets/assign-to-countries.md).

**Customer rule**\
A rule limiting an option set to particular customers — by tag, by account, or to logged-in visitors only. See [Assign to customers](../option-sets/assign-to-customers.md).

## D

**Default value**\
A pre-filled answer. If it carries an add-on price, that price is charged from page load — the most common cause of "my product looks more expensive than I listed it". See [Required field and default value](../option-types/shared-settings/required-and-default-value.md#default-value).

**Dimension**\
An option type collecting two or more measurements and pricing them with a formula. See [Dimension](../option-types/input-types/dimension.md).

**Draft**\
An option set's inactive status. Draft sets never render on the storefront. See [Status and sales channels](../concepts/status-and-sales-channels.md).

## F

**Formula** (dimension)\
The expression turning measurements into a price, using multiplication, addition, and division. Subtraction is not allowed. See [Dimension add-on formula](../add-on-pricing/dimension-formula.md).

## H

**Help text**\
Guidance shown under an option's label. See [Placeholder and help text](../option-types/shared-settings/placeholder-and-help-text.md).

**Hidden label**\
A setting that keeps an option's label out of the rendered page while retaining it in the app. See [Labels and visibility](../option-types/shared-settings/labels-and-visibility.md).

## I

**Input type**\
An option type where the customer types, picks, or uploads something rather than choosing from your list — Text, Number, File upload, Date, and others. See [Input types](../option-types/input-types/README.md).

## L

**Label**\
What the shopper reads. Distinct from **Name**. See [Label vs Name](../concepts/label-vs-name.md).

**Layer** (Personalizer)\
One element drawn on the preview canvas — text from a text option, or an image from a file upload or an image swatch. See [Text layers](../personalizer/text-layers.md) and [Image layers](../personalizer/image-layers.md).

**Line item property**\
The mechanism Shopify uses to carry extra information on a cart line. Every option a customer fills in becomes one. See [Line item properties](../reference/line-item-properties.md).

**Live preview**\
The builder's panel showing your option set as a shopper would see it, updating as you edit. See [Live preview and inspector](../option-sets/live-preview-and-inspector.md).

## M

**Match theme style**\
A setting that borrows your theme's fonts, colours, and control styling so the widget looks native. See [Match your theme style](../storefront/match-your-theme-style.md).

**Merge as bundle**\
Presenting the main product and its add-on products as one cart item rather than several lines. See [Merge main product and add-ons](../add-on-pricing/merge-as-bundle.md).

## N

**Name**\
What appears on the cart, the order, and your paperwork. Distinct from **Label**. See [Label vs Name](../concepts/label-vs-name.md).

## O

**One time charge**\
The add-on mode charging once per order, whatever the quantity. What gift wrap and delivery upgrades should almost always use.

**Option**\
A single question in an option set — one field, one swatch grid, one date picker.

**Option set**\
A group of options assigned to products, with its own status, rules, and sales channels. The unit you build and assign. See [Option set, option, value](../concepts/option-set-option-value.md).

**Option type**\
What kind of control an option is. Thirty-two of them, in three families: input, selection, and static. See [Option types](../option-types/README.md).

**Option value**\
One choice within a selection option — one colour, one size. Values carry their own prices, images, and stock. See [Option values](../concepts/option-values.md).

**Out of stock options**\
The setting deciding what shoppers see when an add-on product runs out — hide it, blur it, or strike it through. Needs a product-backed add-on and inventory tracking. See [Out of stock options](../option-types/shared-settings/out-of-stock-options.md).

## P

**Per character**\
The add-on mode charging by how many characters were typed. Text and Textarea only.

**Personalizer**\
The live preview drawing customer text and images onto your product photo. See [Personalizer](../personalizer/README.md).

**Placeholder**\
Grey prompt text inside an empty field, which disappears as soon as the customer types. Not a substitute for help text. See [Placeholder and help text](../option-types/shared-settings/placeholder-and-help-text.md).

**Product rule**\
How an option set decides which products it applies to — all products, specific products, specific collections, or an automatic rule. See [Assign to products](../option-sets/assign-to-products.md).

## Q

**Quickview**\
A theme popup showing a product without leaving the page. Options can render inside it. See [Quickview and other pages](../storefront/quickview-and-other-pages.md).

## R

**Required**\
An option that must be answered before the product can be added to the cart — unless it is hidden by a conditional rule. See [Required field and default value](../option-types/shared-settings/required-and-default-value.md).

## S

**Sales channel** (option set)\
Whether the option set applies to the Online Store, Point of Sale, or both. See [Status and sales channels](../concepts/status-and-sales-channels.md).

**Section**\
A visual group inside an option set, used to break long forms into headed blocks. Sections can have their own conditional logic. See [Build your options](../option-sets/build-options.md#sections).

**Selection type**\
An option type where the customer chooses from values you defined — Radio button, Checkbox, Dropdown, Color swatch, and others. See [Selection types](../option-types/selection-types/README.md).

**Static type**\
An option type that displays something rather than collecting an answer — a heading, a paragraph, a divider, an image. See [Static types](../option-types/static-types/README.md).

**Swatch**\
A visual value — a colour block, a product image, an uploaded image, or a variant image. See [Swatch style and previews](../option-types/shared-settings/swatch-style-and-previews.md).

## T

**Template** (option set)\
A ready-made option set you can start from instead of building from scratch. See [Templates](../templates/README.md).

**Translation**\
A per-locale version of your option text and the widget's own wording. See [Translations](../translations/README.md).

## V

**Variant**\
Shopify's own product variation — size, colour, material — chosen in your theme's picker, not in the app's widget. The app's options sit alongside variants and can react to them. See [Conditions on Shopify variants](../conditional-logic/conditions-on-shopify-variants.md).

## W

**Widget**\
The form the app renders on your storefront: your options, laid out and styled. See [Storefront widget](../storefront/README.md).

**Widget placement**\
Where on the product page the widget appears. See [Widget placement](../storefront/widget-placement.md).

**Workflow** (automation)\
A rule that acts on orders containing app options — tagging them, or writing the options into the order note. See [Automations](../automations/README.md).
