# Overview

The app renders inside your theme, on your product pages, alongside whatever else you have installed. Most of the time that just works. This section covers the cases where it needs a hand.

## The general principle

The app needs three things to work on a page:

<table><thead><tr><th width="60">#</th><th width="230">It needs</th><th>Which means</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>1</td><td>The app embed enabled on the theme</td><td>Store-wide, once per theme. See <a href="../getting-started/enable-the-app-embed.md">Enable the app embed</a></td></tr><tr><td>2</td><td>A product in context</td><td>A product page, or a featured product section. A general page with no product has nothing to attach options to</td></tr><tr><td>3</td><td>A place to render, and a form to attach to</td><td>Automatic placement finds your theme's product form. Where another tool builds the page instead, an <a href="../getting-started/add-the-app-block.md">app block</a> or a CSS selector may be needed</td></tr></tbody></table>

When something does not render, it is almost always the second or third of those.

## When to contact support instead of reading on

Theme and app integration is the most common thing our support team does, and much of it is a small piece of work rather than a setting you have missed.

Worth asking about:

* Options not appearing in a quickview or a sticky add-to-cart bar
* [Conditions based on Shopify variants](../conditional-logic/conditions-on-shopify-variants.md) not firing on your theme
* Options not rendering on a page built with a page builder
* Anything that works on one theme and not on another

Include your theme name, the app involved, and a link to the page. See [Contact support](../help/contact-support.md).
