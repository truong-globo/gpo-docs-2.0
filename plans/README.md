# Overview

Open **Pricing** in the app to see the plans available to your store, compare what each one includes, and switch.

The app is usable on a free plan. Paid plans add option types, configuration, and features, and each comes with a 14-day free trial. Everything the app can do is visible whatever plan you are on — features your plan does not include are shown but locked, with an upgrade prompt, so you can see what you would be paying for.

## Notes

* Billing goes through Shopify. Charges appear on your Shopify invoice, not separately.
* This documentation does not print prices: they differ by pricing version and for Shopify Plus stores, so the **Pricing** page in the app is the only reliable source.
* On a new install the app opens on **Pricing** and asks you to choose a plan — the free plan counts.
