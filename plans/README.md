# Overview

The app is available on a free plan and several paid ones. Everything the app can do is visible whatever plan you are on — features your plan does not include are shown but locked, so you can see what upgrading would give you before paying for it.

## Where to look

The **Pricing** page in the app is the authoritative list for your store. It shows the plans available to you, marks your current one, and lists every feature with what each plan gives you.

This documentation deliberately does not print prices: they differ by pricing version and for Shopify Plus stores, so the in-app page is the only reliable source.

## Notes

* Billing goes through Shopify. Charges appear on your Shopify invoice, not separately.
* Starting a paid plan or a trial requires Shopify's own charge approval.
* On a new install the app opens on **Pricing** and requires you to choose a plan — the free plan counts.
* Locked features are visible with an upgrade prompt rather than hidden. See [Locked features](locked-features.md).
