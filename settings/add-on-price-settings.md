# Add-on price settings

**Settings** > **Settings** > **Add-on price**. Seven settings, store-wide, covering how add-on charges are *presented*. What is actually charged is set on the options themselves.

The settings are documented in full under [Add-on price display settings](../add-on-pricing/price-display-settings.md). This page is the summary.

## The two that change the experience most

**Add add-on price to the product price** decides whether shoppers see one running total or a base price plus extras. With it on, and an option carrying a priced [default value](../option-types/shared-settings/required-and-default-value.md#default-value), the product looks more expensive than your listed price the moment the page loads — the most common cause of "why is this more than advertised?".

**Merge Main product & Add-on products** decides whether the cart reads as one customised item or as an itemised list. Merging suits heavily configured products; separate lines suit add-ons that are substantial items in their own right. See [Merge main product and add-ons](../add-on-pricing/merge-as-bundle.md).
