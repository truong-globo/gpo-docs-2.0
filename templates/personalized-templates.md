# Personalized templates

Twenty complete setups that include the [Product Personalizer](../personalizer/README.md): the options, the background, and the layers already positioned.

They are the quickest way to a working personalised product, and the clearest way to learn how the Personalizer is put together.

The full list of the twenty, and the step-by-step process for adapting one to your own products, is on the Personalizer page dedicated to them:

**→ [Personalized templates](../personalizer/personalized-templates.md)**

## The one thing to know

A template's layers are positioned against **its own demo photograph**. Your product photograph is framed differently, so the layers will not line up until you:

1. Change the background to your own product
2. Reposition every layer against it

That is not a fault — it is unavoidable, since positions are relative to the image. It is still far less work than building from scratch.

See [Set the preview background](../personalizer/set-the-background.md).

## Notes

* Personalized templates need the Personalizer on your plan. Without it the option set is created but nothing is drawn on the product photo.
* Like all templates, they arrive as **Draft** with no product rule.
* Once adapted, save your version as a [custom template](custom-templates.md).
